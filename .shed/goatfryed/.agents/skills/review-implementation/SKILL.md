# Review
Review the requested change and briefly summarize your understanding of its intent.

Report material findings with their triage, impact and evidence.

Also verify compliance of system contracts within the change.

## Triage
Use triage categories nit, smell, risk, gap, bug.

Style errors must violate explicitly documented project conventions.
